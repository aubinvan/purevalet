# Code of Conduct

PureValet follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome to use, discuss and improve PureValet, whatever their experience, background, identity or the language they write in (English and French are both fine here).

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not about people.
- Keep discussions on topic and respect maintainers' decisions on scope.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory or sexualised language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously do.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/aubinvan). Reports are handled confidentially.
